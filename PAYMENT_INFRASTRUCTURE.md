# MOVETI Socials Payment Infrastructure

## Current status

Payment infrastructure is prepared in TEST mode.

### Subscription plans

- 5 Months — K40,000 — Unlimited releases
- 1 Year — K100,000 — Unlimited releases

### Artist earnings

- Artist royalties: 100%
- MOVETI royalty commission: 0%

### Wallet architecture

Artist balances remain separate from MOVETI business finances.

Artists can request withdrawals through:
- Airtel Money
- TNM Mpamba
- Bank Account

MOVETI does not store mobile-money PINs.

## Live activation

Live payment collection and payouts remain disabled until:
1. Payment provider compliance is approved.
2. Live API credentials are supplied.
3. Webhook/signature verification is configured.
4. Production testing is completed.

No real money is moved by this configuration.
